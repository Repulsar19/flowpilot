--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 This procedure defines how Meridian Biopharma identifies, assesses, approves and implements
    changes arising from new or revised external regulatory requirements (e.g. FDA guidance, EMA
    guidelines, ICH harmonised guidelines, national pharmacopoeia updates) so that the Pharmaceutical
    Quality System (PQS) remains in a continual state of compliance.

1.2 The procedure ensures that regulatory changes are evaluated for impact, risk-classified, approved
    by the appropriate authority, implemented through controlled documentation and training, and
    verified for effectiveness, in accordance with the change management system expectations of
    ICH Q10 Section 3.2.3.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. SCOPE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 This SOP applies to all externally originated regulatory updates that may affect GxP activities
    at Meridian Biopharma manufacturing, quality control and regulatory affairs functions at the
    Northbridge and Eastfield sites.

2.2 Internally originated changes (e.g. equipment modifications, process improvements) are handled
    under SOP-QA-018 (Change Control) and are outside the scope of this document.

2.3 Approximately 35 to 45 external regulatory notifications are received per week across all
    monitored sources. Historically, approximately 8 percent are determined to be applicable to
    Meridian operations.

4. DEFINITIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 Regulatory Update: Any new, revised or withdrawn regulation, guidance, guideline, pharmacopoeial
    monograph or health authority communication that may affect GxP activities.
4.2 Applicability Determination: A documented decision on whether a Regulatory Update affects
    Meridian products, processes, sites or systems.
4.3 Impact Assessment (IA): A structured evaluation identifying affected SOPs, processes, products,
    systems, registrations and training.
4.4 Risk Classification: Categorisation of the change as Low, Medium or High based on potential
    impact on product quality, patient safety and data integrity, applying ICH Q9 principles.
4.5 Change Request (CR): The controlled record in the electronic Quality Management System (eQMS)
    that documents the proposed change, approvals and implementation evidence.
4.6 Change Control Board (CCB): The cross-functional forum that approves Medium and High risk
    changes. The CCB meets weekly on Tuesdays.
4.7 Regulatory Intelligence Log (RIL): The controlled spreadsheet used to record all incoming
    Regulatory Updates and their applicability determinations.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 Regulatory Affairs (RA) Specialist
    - Monitors designated regulatory sources daily
    - Logs Regulatory Updates in the RIL
    - Performs Initial Review and Applicability Determination
    - Drafts the Regulatory Summary

5.2 Regulatory Affairs Manager
    - Reviews applicability rationale for updates determined "Not Applicable"
    - Co-reviews filing implications during Compliance Review
    - Approves regulatory interpretation

5.3 Quality Assurance (QA) Analyst
    - Coordinates the Impact Assessment with Subject Matter Experts (SMEs)
    - Consolidates SME feedback into the IA form (FRM-QA-042-01)
    - Performs Implementation Verification and closes the CR

5.4 Subject Matter Experts (SMEs)
    - Provide functional impact input (Manufacturing, QC Laboratory, Validation, Supply Chain, IT)
    - Respond to IA requests within 5 business days

5.5 QA Manager
    - Assigns Risk Classification
    - Determines the approval pathway
    - Chairs the CCB

5.6 Process Owner
    - Creates the Change Request in the eQMS
    - Owns implementation of approved actions

5.7 Compliance Officer
    - Performs Compliance Review of the proposed change
    - Confirms alignment with internal policies and data integrity requirements

5.8 Head of Quality and Site Head
    - Approve High risk changes (dual electronic signature, 21 CFR Part 11 compliant)

5.9 Document Control Specialist
    - Revises and issues controlled documents in the Document Management System (DMS)

5.10 Training Coordinator
    - Assigns and tracks training in the Learning Management System (LMS)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. PROCEDURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

6.1 Receipt and Logging of Regulatory Update
    6.1.1 The RA Specialist shall review the following sources each business day: FDA Federal
          Register notices and guidance pages, EMA news and scientific guidelines, ICH
          announcements, USP/EP pharmacopoeial forum publications, and the subscribed regulatory
          intelligence e-mail digest.
    6.1.2 Each potentially relevant item shall be entered in the RIL within 2 business days of
          publication, recording: source, publication date, title, URL, document type, and
          issuing authority.
    6.1.3 The RA Specialist shall download the source document and store it in the RA shared drive
          folder "Regulatory Intelligence / <Year> / <Month>".
    6.1.4 Typical effort: 15 to 25 minutes per item. Approximately 40 items per week.
    Inputs: Regulatory source publications; e-mail digest.
    Outputs: RIL entry; archived source document.
    Systems: RIL (Excel), RA shared drive, e-mail.

6.2 Initial Review and Applicability Determination
    6.2.1 For each logged item, the RA Specialist shall read the full source document and determine
          whether it applies to any Meridian product, process, site, registration or system.
    6.2.2 The applicability rationale shall be recorded in the RIL. Items determined "Not Applicable"
          shall be reviewed and countersigned by the RA Manager within 5 business days.
    6.2.3 Items determined "Applicable" shall proceed to 6.3. The RA Specialist shall prepare a
          Regulatory Summary (maximum 2 pages) describing the change, its effective/compliance
          date and the sections of the regulation affected.
    6.2.4 Typical effort: 2 to 4 hours per item for full review; 1 hour for the Regulatory Summary.
    Decision: Applicable / Not Applicable.
    Inputs: Source document; RIL entry; product and site registration list.
    Outputs: Applicability rationale; Regulatory Summary (FRM-RA-042-02); RA Manager countersign.
    Systems: RIL (Excel), RA shared drive, e-mail.

6.3 Impact Assessment
    6.3.1 The QA Analyst shall open an Impact Assessment form (FRM-QA-042-01) and identify
          candidate affected areas using the SOP master list, the product register and the
          validated systems inventory.
    6.3.2 The QA Analyst shall send the Regulatory Summary and IA form to the relevant SMEs by e-mail
          requesting functional impact input within 5 business days.
    6.3.3 SMEs shall identify affected SOPs, batch records, specifications, validation documents,
          registrations and training curricula in their area and estimate implementation effort.
    6.3.4 The QA Analyst shall consolidate responses, resolve conflicts through follow-up meetings,
          and finalise the IA. Where SME responses are not received within 5 business days the QA
          Analyst shall escalate to the functional manager.
    6.3.5 Typical elapsed time: 7 to 12 business days. Typical QA Analyst effort: 6 to 10 hours.
          Average of 3.2 follow-up e-mails per SME per assessment.
    Inputs: Regulatory Summary; SOP master list; product register; validated systems inventory.
    Outputs: Completed IA form listing affected documents, processes, products and systems.
    Systems: E-mail, DMS (read), SOP master list (Excel), eQMS (read).

6.4 Risk Classification
    6.4.1 The QA Manager shall review the completed IA and assign a Risk Classification:
          - LOW: Administrative or editorial; no impact on product quality, patient safety or
            registered details (e.g. reference updates, terminology alignment).
          - MEDIUM: Affects procedures or controls but no change to registered details or critical
            process parameters.
          - HIGH: Affects registered details, critical quality attributes, critical process
            parameters, data integrity controls, or requires a regulatory submission.
    6.4.2 The QA Manager shall document the classification rationale on the IA form.
    6.4.3 The approval pathway shall be determined as follows:
          - LOW: Process Owner and QA Manager approval.
          - MEDIUM: CCB approval.
          - HIGH: CCB approval plus Head of Quality and Site Head electronic signatures.
    6.4.4 Typical effort: 1 to 2 hours. Typical wait for QA Manager availability: 2 to 3 business days.
    Decision: Low / Medium / High risk; approval pathway.
    Inputs: Completed IA form.
    Outputs: Risk Classification and rationale; approval pathway.
    Systems: IA form (Word/PDF), e-mail.

6.5 Change Request Creation
    6.5.1 The Process Owner shall create a Change Request in the eQMS, completing all 14 mandatory
          fields including: change title, description, justification, regulatory reference, risk
          classification, affected documents, affected products, affected systems, proposed
          implementation date, training impact, validation impact, and regulatory filing impact.
    6.5.2 The Process Owner shall attach the Regulatory Summary, the IA form and the source
          regulatory document to the CR.
    6.5.3 The Process Owner shall define implementation action items with owners and due dates.
    6.5.4 Typical effort: 1.5 to 2.5 hours, of which approximately 60 percent is re-entering
          information already present in the IA form and Regulatory Summary.
    Inputs: Regulatory Summary; IA form; Risk Classification.
    Outputs: CR record in eQMS with attachments and action plan.
    Systems: eQMS.

6.6 Compliance and Regulatory Review
    6.6.1 The Compliance Officer shall review the CR to confirm: the regulatory interpretation is
          correct; internal policies are aligned; data integrity requirements (ALCOA+) are
          addressed; and the proposed actions are complete.
    6.6.2 The RA Manager shall assess whether the change requires a regulatory submission
          (e.g. variation, supplement, notification) and document the filing strategy.
    6.6.3 Review comments shall be returned to the Process Owner via the eQMS. The CR shall not
          proceed to approval until all comments are closed.
    6.6.4 Typical elapsed time: 3 to 5 business days. On average 1.6 review cycles are required.
    Inputs: CR record with attachments.
    Outputs: Compliance review record; filing strategy; closed review comments.
    Systems: eQMS, regulatory submissions tracker.

6.7 Change Control Board Approval
    6.7.1 Medium and High risk CRs shall be presented at the weekly CCB meeting by the Process Owner.
    6.7.2 The CCB shall approve, reject, or return the CR with conditions. Decisions and rationale
          shall be recorded in the eQMS by the QA Manager.
    6.7.3 High risk CRs shall additionally require electronic signatures from the Head of Quality
          and the Site Head in the eQMS in accordance with 21 CFR Part 11.
    6.7.4 Low risk CRs are approved electronically by the Process Owner and QA Manager without
          CCB presentation.
    6.7.5 Typical wait for the next CCB meeting: average 4.5 business days. Approximately 20 percent
          of CRs are returned for additional information and re-presented the following week.
    Decision: Approve / Reject / Return with conditions.
    Inputs: Reviewed CR; CCB agenda.
    Outputs: Approval decision with electronic signatures; CCB minutes.
    Systems: eQMS, meeting minutes (Word), e-mail.

6.8 Documentation Update
    6.8.1 The Document Control Specialist shall initiate document revisions in the DMS for each
          affected controlled document listed in the CR.
    6.8.2 The document author shall prepare redlined revisions incorporating the regulatory
          requirement. The Document Control Specialist shall verify formatting, numbering and
          cross-references per SOP-DC-003.
    6.8.3 Revised documents shall be routed for review and approval in the DMS. Approvers include the
          document owner, QA, and functional heads as defined in the document's approval matrix.
    6.8.4 The Document Control Specialist shall set the effective date, typically 30 days after
          approval to allow for training, and shall archive superseded versions.
    6.8.5 Typical elapsed time: 10 to 15 business days per document set. Typical Document Control
          effort: 3 to 4 hours per document. Average of 4 documents affected per regulatory change.
    Inputs: Approved CR; current controlled documents.
    Outputs: Approved revised documents with effective dates; superseded versions archived.
    Systems: DMS, eQMS (status update).

6.9 Training Assignment and Completion
    6.9.1 The Training Coordinator shall identify affected personnel using the curriculum matrix in
          the LMS and assign training on the revised documents.
    6.9.2 Training shall be completed before the document effective date. Read-and-understand
          training is acceptable for Low and Medium risk changes; instructor-led training with
          assessment is required for High risk changes.
    6.9.3 The Training Coordinator shall monitor completion weekly and send reminders. Completion
          below 95 percent at 5 days before the effective date shall be escalated to the functional
          manager.
    6.9.4 Typical elapsed time: 20 to 30 calendar days. Typical Training Coordinator effort: 4 to 6
          hours per change including reminders and reporting.
    Inputs: Approved revised documents; curriculum matrix.
    Outputs: Training assignments; completion records; escalation notices.
    Systems: LMS, e-mail.

6.10 Implementation Verification and Change Closure
    6.10.1 The QA Analyst shall verify that all CR action items are complete, all affected
           documents are effective, training completion is at least 95 percent, and any regulatory
           submissions have been filed.
    6.10.2 For High risk changes, an effectiveness check shall be scheduled 90 days after
           implementation and recorded in the eQMS.
    6.10.3 The QA Analyst shall close the CR in the eQMS. The RIL entry shall be updated with the
           CR number and closure date.
    6.10.4 Typical effort: 2 to 3 hours. Typical elapsed time: 2 to 4 business days.
    Inputs: CR record; DMS status; LMS completion report; submissions tracker.
    Outputs: Closed CR; effectiveness check schedule; updated RIL.
    Systems: eQMS, DMS, LMS, RIL (Excel).

7. RECORDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7.1 The following records shall be retained per 21 CFR 211.180 and the site retention schedule:
    Regulatory Intelligence Log; Regulatory Summary (FRM-RA-042-02); Impact Assessment form
    (FRM-QA-042-01); Change Request and approvals (eQMS); CCB minutes; revised controlled documents
    (DMS); training records (LMS); effectiveness check records.
7.2 Electronic records and signatures shall comply with 21 CFR Part 11, including audit trails
    that capture the identity of the person, the date and time, and the action performed.
